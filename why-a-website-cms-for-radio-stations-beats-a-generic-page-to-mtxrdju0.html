The Four Layers Summed Up

Keep the shape as four connected layers. Layer one is the foundation: a clean, spellable domain that listeners recall. Layer two is the site: a home base where the schedule lives. Traps That Split a Station

The trap most new owners hit is stitching the pieces from incompatible services. A site from one place, the app from another, the audio from a third: what you get is friction. The audio drops, the site drags, and the app falls out of sync. A close runner-up is picking fonts before fixing the flow. Colors are easy; keeping every layer in step is what counts. Resist the quick fix, start from a single base, and the fragmentation never sets in.

Plan the Whole Stack Before You Build a Piece

It helps to plan the whole set before you touch a single tool. List the name you like and two backups, because the perfect one is sometimes already taken. Figure out which features your show really uses, so the hub goes live tidy instead of messy. Map out where your audience already spends time, and let that steer which layer you build next. A station that plans the stack saves time, launches cleaner, and scales in a straight line.
